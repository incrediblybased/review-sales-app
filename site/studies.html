Six pieces of work

Studies

Studies 1 to 3 asked whether reviews predict next quarter's sales direction. Study 4 asked whether review counts estimate the quarter that just ended, before the company reports it. Study 5 combined every signal in one model. Study 6 repeats study 4 on new, PC-focused publishers that report every six months.
