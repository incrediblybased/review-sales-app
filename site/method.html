How the work was done

Method

Every test was written down and frozen, with file fingerprints, before any sales figure for its companies was fetched. Companies were chosen by written rules about data availability, never by their results.

Data

Amazon reviews
McAuley-Lab Amazon Reviews 2023 (research dataset)
Steam reviews
113.9M reviews to Nov 2023 (CC BY-NC 4.0)
Game publishers
Steam games metadata, 124,146 games (MIT)
Sales
SEC filings, Japanese results summaries, Nordic interim reports, UK results announcements, company releases
Tone model
nlptown BERT sentiment model, run offline

Rules that protect the result

  • Sales figures as first reported, or the prior-year figure printed in the same report.
  • Each company scored by a model fitted only on other companies.
  • Two simple rules as the bar to beat; three-part pass mark fixed in advance.
  • Every quarter's prior-year figure cross-checked against the figure reported a year earlier.
  • No paid data, no logging in, no getting around any website's blocks.
