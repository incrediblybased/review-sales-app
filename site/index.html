Amazon and Steam reviews · company-reported revenue · 2014 to 2023

Can customer reviews tell us how a company's sales are doing?

Reviews are public, written every day and come from buyers. Company sales are published weeks after each quarter ends. This project tested, in several ways and with the rules written down before any sales figures were looked at, whether reviews give an earlier read on sales.

Every study at a glance

Each row compares the review-based approach with a simple rule that uses only the company's own recent sales growth.

What this shows

  • Whether review mood or review volume points the way on reported sales, for one company at a time and for many companies together.
  • How every approach compares with two simple rules, scored on companies and years the model did not see.
  • Where reviews and sales do move together: mostly for publishers whose business sits on Steam.

What it does not show

  • It is not investment advice and does not test share prices or analyst expectations.
  • Reviews cover one channel each (Amazon US, Steam). Console, mobile and shop sales are invisible.
  • Review histories are 2023 snapshots rebuilt after the fact.
